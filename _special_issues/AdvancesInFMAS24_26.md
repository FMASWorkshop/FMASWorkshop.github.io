Science of Computer Programming special issue <br>

Advances in Formal Methods for Autonomous Systems<br> 2024 &ndash; 2026
</h3>
Autonomy, or the ability for a system to make decisions without human intervention, enables the system to react flexibly to its environment. Autonomous systems need careful verification to ensure that their behaviour is safe, secure, and free of other ethical harms. Autonomous systems present unique challenges for formal methods, including: modelling the system's dynamic environment; verifying the system's decision-making capabilities; and using formal methods results as evidence for certification or regulation. 

Since 2019, the **International Workshop on Formal Methods for Autonomous Systems (FMAS)** has provided a venue that brings together researchers in this intersectional domain to present and discuss their work.

_This special issue invites_ authors from the 2024, 2025, and 2026 editions of the workshop, and new work that has not featured in the workshop, to showcase the advances of formal methods for autonomous systems over the past three years.
